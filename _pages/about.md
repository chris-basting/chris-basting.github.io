I'm a computational biologist who studies how the microbiome, metabolism, and the immune system interact in human disease. I recently completed my PhD in Bioinformatics and Computational Biology at the University of Minnesota, where my dissertation investigated the drivers of chronic inflammation in people with HIV on antiretroviral therapy.
