Plasma metabolome profiling of people with HIV on antiretroviral therapy and healthy controls, with a focus on immunological non-responders, whose CD4 T cell counts recover poorly despite sustained viral suppression. The aim is to identify interventions that could reduce chronic inflammation and related comorbidities.
